## Project relevance, costs and limitations

## Reproduction or A/B experiment

Define control/variant, fixed inputs/configuration, sample/repetition method, metrics, correctness guardrails, adoption/rejection criteria and artifact locations. Reuse an existing harness where suitable; disclose its coverage gaps. Record exact tested commit/diff and environment. Do not imply the plan ran.

## Outcome and follow-up
