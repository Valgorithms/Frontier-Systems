retains canonical paid records and save version 9. No authority, conservation,
reach, rectangle, plane or principal contract changes are proposed.

Observation version 10 adds a bounded own-panel projection with safe descriptors
and revision-coherent geometric closure feedback. Private principals, receipts,
payment records and grants stay on the host. Versions 7 through 9 remain readable.

Collapsed contextual controls offer deliberate floor/ceiling placement, 50 mm
coordinates and dimensions, area cost, stable own-panel selection and removal.

not movement collision. Closure feedback is aggregate geometric status/revision.

malformed/foreign/stale projection controls, finite debit/refund/retry, restored
records, legacy observations and existing wall/canister regressions. Local Godot
execution requires an exclusive Coordinator lease.

uses all canonical walls/panels through the existing owner query. Own-only
descriptors cannot reconstruct that result; no foreign surface IDs are projected.
